The Six Decisions (Fixed Shape, Any Order)
  1. Two ledgers, one decision: the policy outcome drives routing; "the thing that has to be consistent is the decision, not the storage".
  2. Sync decision, async everything else: the scheme response budget forced it.
  3. Who owns the timeout: the client, and it resolves to a defined fallback.
  4. Idempotency on the domain identifier: enforced where duplicates are cheapest to stop.
  5. Tech stack, flagged as design reasoning: designed and defended, not operated. Say which is which.
  6. Canonical at the contract, autonomous behind it: shared meaning, not shared database.
